Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Publisher: Frictionborn · App: Burstdown: Bottle Stair Smash (com.frictionborn.burst)

Players of Burstdown (Burstdown: Bottle Stair Smash, published by Frictionborn) can permanently delete their entire account and associated personal data at any time — or request deletion of specific data without deleting their whole account — whether using an anonymous Guest account or a linked Google / Google Play Games account.

Option 1 — Instant In-App Full Account Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have Burstdown installed on your Android device:

  1. Open Burstdown.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  3. Scroll to the Account & Privacy section and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account, cloud save, and linked identifiers are queued for immediate erasure and local save data is wiped.

Option 2 — Request Full Account Deletion by Email (Without the App)

If you have already uninstalled Burstdown or cannot access the app, you can request full account and data deletion by emailing our support team directly:

Email Full Account Deletion Request

Or send an email to hunainibnshakeel@gmail.com with the subject line Burstdown Account Deletion Request and include either:

  • Your Player ID / Support ID (shown at the bottom of the in-game Settings screen), or
  • The Google account email address you linked to Burstdown.

Option 3 — Delete Specific Data Without Deleting Your Whole Account (Partial Deletion)

If you want to delete only some of your data while keeping your Burstdown account, inventory, and gameplay progress active, you can request partial data deletion by email:

Email Partial Data Deletion Request

Send an email to hunainibnshakeel@gmail.com with the subject line Burstdown Partial Data Deletion Request, your Player ID / Support ID (or linked Google email), and specify which items you want removed (for example: published Can Design Studio labels, ghost recordings, leaderboard scores, first-party analytics events, or linked Google identity).

What Data Is Deleted Upon Full Account Deletion

  • Your Supabase user account (anonymous UUID and/or linked Google Sign-In & Google Play Games identifiers)
  • Cloud save progress, inventory, wallet balances, wallet ledger, and player XP/level
  • First-party analytics events associated with your user ID
  • Leaderboard scores, ghost recordings, duel history, and friend connections
  • Designs published to the Can Design Studio online gallery

What Data Is Retained and Why

Retained Record Why It Is Retained Retention Period
Mystery Crate draw logs (crate_opens) Required to comply with randomized item audit and probability disclosure regulations. Kept for at least 90 days; after account deletion they are kept only without the account link (user_id set to NULL) and deleted once 90 days old (via our nightly purge_old_draw_logs job).
De-identified purchase & tax records Required by applicable tax, accounting, and financial audit laws via Google Play Billing. Retained for the statutory tax retention period with personal profile identifiers removed.

Deletion Timeline

In-app deletion requests take effect immediately and complete across all backend tables and backups within 30 days. Email requests (for either full account deletion or partial data deletion) are verified and completed within 30 days of receipt, and we send a confirmation email once erasure is complete.
