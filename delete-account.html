Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Publisher: Frictionborn · App: Burstdown: Bottle Stair Smash (com.frictionborn.burst)

Players of Burstdown (Burstdown: Bottle Stair Smash, published by Frictionborn) can permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time — whether using an anonymous Guest account or a linked Google / Google Play Games account.

Option 1 — Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have Burstdown installed on your Android device:

  1. Open Burstdown.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  3. Scroll to the Account & Privacy section and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account, cloud save, and linked identifiers are queued for immediate erasure and local save data is wiped.

Option 2 — Request Deletion Without the App (Web / Email Route)

If you have already uninstalled Burstdown or cannot access the app, you can request full account and data deletion by emailing our support team directly:

Email Account Deletion Request

Or send an email to hunainibnshakeel@gmail.com with the subject line Burstdown Account Deletion Request and include either:

  • Your Player ID / Support ID (shown at the bottom of the in-game Settings screen), or
  • The Google account email address you linked to Burstdown.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your Supabase user account (anonymous UUID and/or linked Google Sign-In & Google Play Games identifiers)
  • Cloud save progress, inventory, wallet balances, and player XP/level
  • Leaderboard scores, ghost recordings, duel history, and friend connections
  • Designs published to the Can Design Studio online gallery

What Data Is Retained and Why

Retained Record Why It Is Retained Retention Period
De-identified Mystery Crate draw logs (crate_opens) Required to comply with randomized item audit and probability disclosure regulations. Upon account deletion, the link to your user account is permanently removed. Automatically purged within 90 days of the draw event.
De-identified purchase & tax records Required by applicable tax, accounting, and financial audit laws via Google Play Billing. Retained for the statutory tax retention period with personal profile identifiers removed.

Deletion Timeline

In-app deletion requests take effect immediately and complete across all backend tables and backups within 30 days. Email deletion requests are verified and completed within 30 days of receipt, and we will send a confirmation email once erasure is complete.
