3. Player Protection & Regional Compliance Rules

  • In-Game Disclosure Before Open (show_odds_before_open: true): These exact percentages and your current pity-timer counter are displayed directly on the crate purchase/open screen inside Burstdown.
  • No "Kompu Gacha" / Set-Completion Prizes (set_completion_rewards: false): There are no rewards or bonuses gated behind collecting a complete set of randomized crate items.
  • Minor Mode Protection (minor_mode_paid_crates: false): Paid mystery crates (such as the Premium Crate) are completely disabled for players in the 13–17 age band.
  • Regional Availability (paid_crates_blocked_countries): Paid mystery crates are disabled in jurisdictions where paid loot boxes are prohibited or restricted by law, including Belgium (BE) and Brazil (BR, Law 15.211/2025 Digital ECA), with remote configuration support for the Netherlands (NL) and EU-wide regulatory updates.
  • Draw Log Retention (draw_log_retention_days: 90): Server-side crate draw logs (crate_opens) are kept for at least 90 days; after account deletion they are kept only without the account link and deleted once 90 days old.
