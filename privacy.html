3. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA, UK, Brazil, India & Global)

  • Performance of a Contract: To provide core gameplay, save your progress, sync entitlements across devices, verify Google Play Billing purchases, and operate leaderboards and multiplayer rooms.
  • Consent: For personalized advertising (requested via the Google User Messaging Platform / IAB TCF consent form before any ad request is made) and optional Google account linking. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain game stability (crash reporting via our processor Sentry), prevent fraud and cheating, moderate user-generated content, and analyze first-party gameplay metrics stored in our own Supabase project.
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with tax/accounting rules for digital purchases, maintain mystery crate draw logs for at least 90 days where required, and enforce regional youth protection and loot-box laws (including Brazil Digital ECA Law 15.211/2025, Belgium gambling rules, and India DPDP).

5. Age Policy, Minors & Youth Protection

  • Target Audience (13+ Only): Burstdown is intended solely for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not participate in the Google Play Families program.
  • Neutral Age Screen (Stores Age Band Only): On first launch, players complete a neutral year-of-birth screen with no default preselected. The age screen stores only an age band (<13, 13–17, or 18+), never your exact birth date.
  • Under-13 Players Blocked: Under-13 players are blocked from playing the game (displaying "This game is for players 13 and older"), and no personal data, analytics, or ad requests are made for them. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected any personal data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.
  • Minor Mode (Ages 13–17) & Regional Protections: For players in the 13–17 age band (and by default where required by regional laws such as Brazil's Digital ECA Law 15.211/2025 and India's DPDP Act):
    • Ads are strictly restricted to non-personalized ads only (npa=1) with maximum content rating set to Teen (T).
    • Paid mystery crates are disabled.
    • Multiplayer communication uses preset emotes only (no free-text or voice chat).
    • Can Design Studio submissions are subject to stricter automated moderation filters, and spending reminders are enabled by default.
  • Play Age Signals API: Where supported by Google Play (e.g., Brazil, Texas, and other applicable jurisdictions), we use the Play Age Signals API solely to confirm age bands for legal compliance — never for advertising or profiling.

7. Data Security

  • Encryption in Transit and at Rest: All data transmitted between the app and our servers or processors is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS (1.2+). All database records, object storage buckets, backups, and diagnostic logs are encrypted at rest by our infrastructure providers (Supabase and Sentry). Local session credentials on your device are stored encrypted.
  • Database Isolation & Access Control: Every Supabase database table is protected by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS), and all currency, entitlement, and leaderboard mutations execute exclusively through authenticated server-side Edge Functions.
  • Restricted Developer Access: Administrative access to backend databases, crash logs, and server secrets is strictly limited to the developer (Frictionborn / Hunain Ahmed) and protected by multi-factor authentication.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in the United States, the European Economic Area (EEA), or other countries where our processors (Supabase, Sentry, Google) operate. When personal data is transferred outside the EEA, UK, Brazil, or India, we rely on European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent statutory data-transfer safeguards.

9. Your Privacy Rights, Full Account Deletion & Partial Data Deletion

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, US states such as California/Virginia/Colorado/Connecticut/Texas, Brazil, and India), you have the following rights:

  • Access & Data Portability: Request a copy of your personal data (or use Settings → Download my data in-game to export your data as JSON).
  • Full Account Deletion: Delete your entire account and personal data at any time via Settings → Delete account in-game, or follow the instructions on our Account Deletion page.
  • Partial Data Deletion (Without Deleting Your Whole Account): You may request deletion of specific data — such as published Can Design Studio labels, ghost recordings, leaderboard scores, first-party analytics events, or linked Google account identifiers — while keeping your Burstdown account and gameplay progress active. To request deletion of some data without deleting the whole account, send an email to hunainibnshakeel@gmail.com with your Player ID / Support ID (found in in-game Settings) and specify the data you want deleted. We fulfill partial deletion requests within 30 days.
  • Correction / Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate account data.
  • Withdraw Consent / Advertising Choices: Open Settings → Privacy options at any time to reopen the Google UMP consent form and withdraw consent for personalized ads, or reset/delete your Advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • US State Opt-Out ("Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"): US residents may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising via Settings → Do not sell or share my info in-game.
  • Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights via email, contact us at hunainibnshakeel@gmail.com.
